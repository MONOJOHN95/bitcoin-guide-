# Welcome to Bitcoin 101

**Your beginner-friendly guide to understanding Bitcoin — the world's first decentralized digital currency.**

---

## What is Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a form of digital money that operates without a central authority. It was invented in 2008 by an unknown person or group using the pseudonym *Satoshi Nakamoto* and released as open-source software in 2009.

- **Decentralized**: No government or institution controls Bitcoin.
- **Secure**: It uses cryptographic proof and a distributed ledger (blockchain).
- **Limited Supply**: Only 21 million bitcoins will ever exist.

---

## How Does Bitcoin Work?

1. **Blockchain**: A public ledger where all transactions are recorded.
2. **Mining**: The process of validating transactions and securing the network.
3. **Wallets**: Digital tools that allow users to send, receive, and store bitcoins.

---

## Why Use Bitcoin?

- **Freedom**: Send money globally, 24/7, without intermediaries.
- **Privacy**: Control over your own financial data.
- **Inflation Resistance**: Fixed supply ensures scarcity.

---

## Getting Started

1. Create a Bitcoin wallet (e.g., [Electrum](https://electrum.org), [BlueWallet](https://bluewallet.io))
2. Buy Bitcoin through exchanges like [Coinbase](https://www.coinbase.com) or [Binance](https://www.binance.com)
3. Learn more on [Bitcoin.org](https://bitcoin.org)
